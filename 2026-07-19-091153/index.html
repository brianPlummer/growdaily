Reading the number: for a single plant in a 2×2 on ~200 W of living soil, a central ~4 oz (~110 g) is a solid-but-modest result, consistent across all three methods. The larfy understory is the main thing holding it out of the higher band. Two adjustments to keep in mind:

  • Wet → dry: freshly cut, trimmed wet flower runs roughly 4–5× the dry weight (buds are ~70–80% water). Expect on the order of ~400–650 g wet trimmed to land in this dried range — do not read a big wet number on the scale as the final yield.
  • Time left: if harvest is genuinely ~1 week out, the tops are still bulking slightly and could add ~5–10% — but only trichome checks can confirm there is real ripening headroom rather than just senescing. Pulling early to "lock it in" would cost more than it saves.
